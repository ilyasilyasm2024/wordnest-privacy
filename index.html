WordNest — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

WordNest ("the app") is a vocabulary-learning app. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in the app — decks, words, translations, examples, notes, study history and settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Pronunciation (text-to-speech)

When you tap a speaker button, the word is read aloud by your device's built-in text-to-speech engine (for example Google Speech Services on Android). The app itself does not send the text anywhere; how your device's speech engine works is governed by its provider's policy.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If future versions add features that send data off the device (for example optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
