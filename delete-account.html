Delete your WordNest account

WordNest works without an account. If you created one (Settings → Account & sync), you can delete it and all its cloud data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open WordNest → Settings → Account & sync.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you no longer have the app, write to ilyas.qb.dev@gmail.com from the address of your account with the subject “Delete my WordNest account”. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account (email address) and everything stored in the cloud for it: decks, words, tags, study progress, review history and the daily AI-use counter. Nothing is kept afterwards. Words saved on your phone stay there until you uninstall the app.
