WordNest — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 (updated for accounts, sync, AI assist, sharing and reminders)

WordNest ("the app") is a vocabulary-learning app. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Without an account (default)

You can use every core feature without an account. Then the app collects nothing: everything you create — decks, words, translations, examples, notes, pictures, study history and settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Optional account and sync

If you choose to sign in (Settings → Account & sync), we use your email address to create your account and send you sign-in codes. Your decks, words (without pictures), tags, study progress and review history are then stored in our cloud database so they can be backed up and synced between your devices. This data is protected so that only your account can read it. We use it only to provide sync; we never sell it or use it for ads. The service is hosted by Supabase (supabase.com) in the region chosen for the project.

Deleting your data

Settings → Account & sync → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all your cloud data. Words on your phone stay until you uninstall the app. You can also ask us by email (below).

AI assist (optional, signed-in users)

When you tap "Suggest translation", "Suggest examples" or "Create a deck with AI", the word or topic you typed and the two languages are sent to our server and to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate the answer. Nothing else from your account is sent. Answers are shown to you and saved only if you choose to save them. We count how many AI requests you make per day to apply a fair-use limit.

AI with your own Google Gemini key (optional)

In Settings → AI assistant you can paste a Google Gemini API key that you create in Google AI Studio. The key is stored only on your device, in its secure storage, and is sent only to Google. When you use the ✨ buttons, the word or topic you typed is sent directly from your device to Google, under Google's terms and privacy policy; it does not go through us. On Gemini's free tier Google may use these requests to improve its products, so do not type private information. Choosing "Off" pauses AI and keeps the key; "Remove key" deletes it from your device.

Reporting AI answers

If you report an AI answer (🚩), we receive the reason, the word or topic you asked about, the reported answer and the app version, so we can improve the results. If you are signed in, the report is linked to your account. Reports contain nothing else.

Sharing a deck

"Send as WordNest file", "Share as PDF" and CSV export create a file on your device that you share with the app you choose; it does not go through us.

Reminders (notifications)

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no data is sent to anyone.

Pronunciation (text-to-speech)

When you tap a speaker button, the word is read aloud by your device's built-in text-to-speech engine (for example Google Speech Services on Android). The app does not send the text anywhere; how your device's speech engine works is governed by its provider's policy.

Camera and photos

You can add a picture to a card. Only then, and only if you choose to, the app asks to use your camera (to take a photo) or opens your photo gallery (to pick one). The picture is resized and saved in the app's private storage on your device; it is never uploaded, even with sync. The app does not access any other photo.

Other permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location or microphone.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Security

Data sent to our server travels encrypted (HTTPS). Secret keys for the server and the AI provider are kept on the server, never in the app.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Accounts are optional; we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent.

Changes

We will update this policy before any new feature changes what data leaves your device, and describe the change in the app's release notes.
